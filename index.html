    Bio

    I shoot landscapes and streets, on digital and film. Most of these were taken in Seoul: back streets, the Han River, early mornings. The rest are from trips.

    I like places best when they're quiet. Studying design probably shapes how I frame things, but mostly I just take pictures of what I like looking at.
